# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community.
Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, welcome people
of different backgrounds and experience levels, and critique work rather than
people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `danil@nddev.it.com`. Maintainers will
review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants based on severity and
history. Good-faith reports must not be retaliated against.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
organization.
